ENLISTED

The foundation of the force and junior leadership track.

06 RANKS
Requirements
- Complete Enlistment Application.
- Pass initial screening.
- Begin with Basic Training pipeline.
Role
Newly inducted personnel developing basic military skills and adapting to KSOL standards.
Requirements
- Complete Basic Training.
- Minimum 15 days time in service.
- Squad Leader / Instructor's recommendation.
Role
Developing soldiers focused on individual proficiency, discipline, and effective participation within their unit.
Requirements
- Minimum 20 days as PVT.
- Passed MOS (Military Occupational Speciality) proficiency.
- At least 1 FTX (Field Training Excercise) attended.
Role
Experienced junior soldiers demonstrating growing competence, reliability, and operational awareness.
Requirements
- Minimum 25 days as PFC.
- Demonstrated MOS proficiency.
- At least 2 FTX attended.
- Commandant recommendation.
Role
Proficient personnel trusted to perform assigned duties independently and contribute specialist knowledge.
Requirements
- Must be an SPC.
- Demonstrate good discipline & leadership qualities in fire team.
- Squad leader recommendation.
- Completed leadership course.
Role
Senior junior personnel beginning to demonstrate initiative and assist in guiding less-experienced soldiers.
Requirements
- Minimum 20 days as LCPL.
- Squad leader recommendation.
Role
Proficient personnel trusted to perform assigned duties independently and contribute specialist knowledge.
NON-COMMISSIONED OFFICERS

Command-supporting NCO leadership ranks.

07 RANKS
Requirements
- Minimum 20 days as CPL.
- At least 3 FTX attended.
- Passed NCO Evaluation Board.
Role
First level of NCO leadership. Leads and supervises a small team or section and is responsible for discipline, basic training, and execution of orders.
Requirements
- Minimum 30 days as SGT.
- Leadership tab earned.
- Demonstrated squad infantry.
- Minimum 8 operations attended.
- Platoon Commander equivalent recommendation.
Role
Junior-to-mid NCO leadership.
Requirements
- Minimum 50 days as SSG.
- Successful squad leader records.
- Leadership tab and Parachute Wings earned.
- Platoon Commander recommendation.
Role
Senior NCO with significant leadership experience.
Requirements
- Minimum 65 days as SFC.
- Platoon Commander recommendation.
Role
Senior NCO primarily focused on unit management, operations, training, or specialist responsibilities.
Requirements
- Minimum 70 days as an SFC.
- Commander, KSOL approval.
Role
Senior NCO responsible for supervising NCO standards, discipline, training, and personnel within a major unit or headquarters.
Requirements
- Must be an SGM.
- Exemplary leadership record.
- Selected by HQ.
- Minimum 100 days in service.
Role
Represents enlisted personnel and advises the commander on discipline, morale, training, readiness, and NCO affairs.
Requirements
- Minimum 20 days as CSM.
- Extraordinary service & leadership.
- Selected by HQ.
- Commander, KSOL approval.
Role
Highest NCO appointment in the Legion. Principal senior enlisted advisor to the Commander of KSOL and responsible for Legion-wide NCO standards, discipline, traditions, and enlisted development.
WARRANT OFFICERS

Technical and specialist leadership appointments.

05 RANKS
Requirements
- Minimum 120 days service.
- At least 25 operations.
- Company Commander equivalent recommendation.
- MOS tab.
Role
Junior warrant officer. A highly experienced technical or tactical specialist who may serve as a senior advisor, instructor, or specialist leader within a company or battalion.
Requirements
- Minimum 10 days as WO1.
- Company Commander equivalent recommendation.
Role
Senior specialist with greater responsibility. May supervise specialist personnel, training programs, or technical functions and advise unit leadership.
Requirements
- Minimum 15 Days as CWO2.
- Company Commander equivalent recommendation.
Role
Experienced senior warrant officer. Can serve at battalion level as a subject-matter expert, operations specialist, or senior technical advisor.
Requirements
- Minimum 15 days as CWO3.
- Company Commander equivalent recommendation.
Role
Senior-level warrant officer responsible for major specialist areas across a battalion or regiment. Advises commanders and mentors junior warrant officers.
Requirements
- Minimum 20 days as CWO4.
- HQ Approval.
Role
Senior technical/warrant appointment.

- Leadership tab. - Passed Officer Evaluation Board.
Role

- Minimum 80 days as 1LT.
- Leadership tab and parachute wings.
Role
Junior company command and officer appointments.
Requirements
- Minimum 50 days as CPT.
- Passed Officer Evaluation Board.
- Battalion Commanding Officer or 2i/c recommendation.
Role
Company-level and staff leadership.
Requirements
- Minimum 100 days as MAJ.
- Regiment Commanding Officer recommendation.
Role
Battalion-level and senior staff command.
Requirements
- Minimum 60 days as LTC.
- Good Command experience.
- Passed Officer Evaluation Board.
Role
Regimental, headquarters, and senior staff command.
Requirements
- HQ appointed.
Role
Senior formation command and high-level appointments.
Requirements
- HQ appointed.
Role
Senior formation and headquarters appointments.
Requirements
- HQ appointed.
Role
Senior command and headquarters appointments.
Requirements
- HQ appointed.
Role
Highest commissioned rank; normally reserved for the senior-most command appointments.
